# Double Battle Rules

A Double Battle is a 2v2 format where each trainer sends out two Pokémon at a time, making it a 4-Pokémon field. Target selection and positioning are key.

## Turn Order Sequence

1. **Check Move Priority Brackets**: Priority always wins, regardless of Speed. The game first groups all four Pokémon by the priority bracket of the move they selected. A higher number always goes first. For example, Protect is +4, Fake Out is +3, Follow Me is +2, standard attacks are 0, and Trick Room is -7.
2. **Compare Speed Stats**: If two or more Pokémon use moves in the same priority bracket (e.g., both use Priority 0 attacks), the game compares their current Speed stats. The higher Speed goes first. If Trick Room is active, this step is reversed, and the lower Speed goes first. However, Trick Room never overrides Step 1 (Priority).
3. **Resolve Speed Ties**: If Pokémon are in the same priority bracket and have the exact same Speed stat, the game literally flips a virtual coin. It is a 50/50 chance who goes first between those tied Pokémon.
4. **Apply Dynamic Recalculation**: Generation 8 onwards. If a Pokémon's Speed changes mid-turn (for example, if it gets paralyzed by a faster opponent's Thunder Wave), the turn order instantly recalculates for the remaining actions. If a Pokémon is now slower than the remaining attackers, it will be bumped down the queue.

## Mechanics
- **Spread Moves**: Moves like Earthquake, Surf, and Discharge hit ALL other Pokémon on the field (including your ally, unless specified otherwise) and do 25% less damage.
- **Single-Target Moves**: You must choose which specific foe (or ally) to attack each turn.
- **Support & Redirection**: Moves like Helping Hand boost your ally's next move by 50%. Follow Me / Rage Powder redirects all attacks to the user.
- **Ally Switch**: Swaps your two active Pokémon's positions.
